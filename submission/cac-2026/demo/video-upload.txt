TITLE
TrueLine — Free, Open-Source Tracing for Makers | Congressional App Challenge

DESCRIPTION
TrueLine is free, open-source tracing software for makers. Import a reference image and begin tracing, review fitted lines and curves, set a known real measurement, and export editable CAD outlines as DXF, SVG or PDF.

The idea began when my dad and I had a physical part without a CAD model. Photo tracing in SolidWorks did not work well for us, and another solution cost about $1,500. I built and refined the tracing functionality and tested it with parts.

The first route traces a complete mounting plate: its asymmetric outer profile, a three-point Circle-tool mounting hole, two fitted bolt holes, and adjustment slot, followed by an 80 mm scale measurement and actual DXF export. The second route demonstrates automatic outline detection: import an overhead image, generate an outline, review it, and set scale before export.

The reference is an original illustration used to demonstrate both workflows. No physical tablet or camera was filmed, and processing waits are shortened. Verify edges and independent physical measurements before fabrication.

The application uses JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Canvas and Electron. Optional auto-outline uses pretrained MobileSAM and ONNX Runtime with TrueLine's tracing/cleanup workflow. The model was not trained or fine-tuned by the student. Projects and processing stay on the user's device.

Development disclosure: I built and refined tracing functionality, including CAD curve fitting, and used my ML knowledge to help choose a small pretrained neural-network approach. Codex and Claude Code assisted implementation, debugging, and testing of much of the app. This video was also produced with AI assistance using actual application interactions, with camera crops, presentation text and timing edits for clarity.
